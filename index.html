Fortress Connected is a co-op and PvP mash-up mod built off of Team Fortress 2. Fight through the world of Half-Life as the Team Fortress 2 cast along with new and old faces!

Featuring:

  • The ability to play through Half-Life 2 and its episodes, Left 4 Dead 1, and more as the TF2 cast alone or with friends!
  • Old and new custom classes based on new designs or familiar faces from other mods!
  • Custom coop campaign experiences with updating support for more in the future!
  • Special features like Grenades, Armour, and more!
  • New weapons, buildables, and achievements!
  • New types of enemies and allies you can find in your adventures.
  • Steam Workshop support for custom mods and maps!
  • Full Half-Life 2: Deathmatch support with a TDM gamemode!
  • Support for your entire TF2 inventory!
  • All this and more to come in the future!
